# UI framework – Formulare

Fields, switches, option cards, click-to-edit, colour picker, upload. Part of the UI framework: read the core rules in [`../FRAMEWORK.md`](../FRAMEWORK.md) first. Live samples: `/design/styleguide-formulare.html`.

## Building blocks

- **Form field** – label **above** the field (orange, semi-bold), a quiet thin frame, 8 px corners, **40 px high with a slim padding – one height for every field in the app** (PO 2026-11-16, as tall as an Umschalter); while typing an orange frame with a soft orange ring; an error gives a thin red frame. The same look for text, password, search, date, number, `select` and `textarea` (grows with its text). Checkboxes, radios, colour and range keep their own look. **Checkbox row (point 217):** `<label><input type="checkbox"> Text</label>` has no top margin (that belongs to a label above a field) and is a compact 36 px row on desktop, the 48 px touch target only under `@media (pointer: coarse)`; a lone checkbox, or the first of a run, after other form parts keeps a .5rem gap, so lists of checkboxes (rights, log books, Belegpaket accounts, filters, dialogs) sit close together. Saved logins (autofill) look like typed text.
  ```html
  <div class="field">
      <label for="username">Benutzername</label>
      <input id="username" name="username" autocomplete="username">
      <p class="field-hint">Optional: a hint below the field.</p>
  </div>
  ```
  An error: `class="field has-error"` (red frame, red hint) or `aria-invalid="true"` on the field. Older forms with the label right before or around the field are drawn the same way; new code uses `.field`.
- **Small field** – `class="input-small"` on an `input` or `select`: since every field is 40 px it only keeps the slightly smaller text, for tight spots (a search above a list, a field in a row). No label above, a placeholder says what goes in (and `aria-label`). Every `type="search"` field is small by itself.
- **Mehrfachauswahl (PO 2026-11-16, variant B)** – `<div class="chips" role="group" aria-label="…"><label class="chip"><input type="checkbox" …><span>Name</span></label>…</div>` (`chips-small` in the search panel, e.g. "Suchen in"): the grey rail of the Umschalter, every field with its own box – grey and empty; ticked, the field lifts white (like a chosen segment) and the box turns orange with a check. No frame, no check text, so a click never changes a width; too narrow, the rail wraps onto a second line (no dropdown – the choices stay in view). **Grid (PO 2026-11-16):** the rail is a grid of equal columns – every field as wide as the group's widest entry (`--chip-w`, measured by `core/button-choice.js` once the group is visible), as many columns as fit; a second line stands right below the first.
- **Checkbox (PO 2026-11-16, variant B2)** – the native `<input type="checkbox">`, no extra markup: a small grey field (30 px) with the box symbol; ticked, the field lifts white and the box turns orange with a check (`--check-off` / `--check-on`). The switch (`role="switch"`) keeps its own look.
- **Frame field** – `<fieldset><legend>Tags</legend>…</fieldset>` for a group (tags, checkboxes, colours): no browser frame, the legend stands above like a field's label.
- **Schalter (switch) – replaced (PO 2026-11-16):** no toggle switch any more. An on/off is an **Umschalter** with two words, e.g. "Status: Aktiv | Pausiert" – it acts at once like before (autosave form; a confirm before switching on stays). The `.switch` CSS stays only until the last app views (Personen, Rollen, Dokumentgröße) are converted, then it goes. Former text: **Schalter (switch, points 218, 223)** – `<label class="switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" name="paused" value="1"> Pausiert</label>` (with a hidden `value="0"` field before it): an on/off that acts at once, inside a `data-autosave` form (Properties module). Off a grey track, on the dark `--secondary` track (never filled orange), white knob; focus ring orange. `data-confirm-title`, `data-confirm-text`, `data-confirm-ok` on the input ask in the framework confirm dialog **before switching on** (`core/switch.js`, capture phase; switching off never asks) – e.g. "Alle 3 Personen mit dieser Rolle werden abgemeldet …". CSS in `core/forms.css`. Use it for states (Pausiert); a list of rights stays checkboxes.
- **Optionskarten (option cards, point 188.1)** – the standard **vertical** choice (source: Einstellungen → Geschwindigkeit, Vorladen / Vorschaubilder): `<fieldset class="pick-list option-cards"><legend>…</legend><label class="pick-row"><input type="radio" class="visually-hidden" …><span><strong>Title</strong><small>Description</small></span><span class="pick-mark"><x-icon name="check" /></span></label>…`: grey rows with title and description, the chosen one in the marked look (white, light shadow, 188.5) with the orange check. A few options with a sentence each = Optionskarten; short words side by side = Umschalter.
- **Aufklappliste (accordion list, points 216, 218)** – the settings list whose records are edited in place (Rollen, Personen): `<ul class="accordion-list">` of `<li class="accordion-row">` with `<div class="accordion-head">` holding **one** `<button type="button" class="list-entry accordion-toggle" data-accordion-toggle aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="…">` (row symbol or Initialen-Kreis, `<span><strong>Name</strong><small>a short line</small></span>`, then `<span class="accordion-pills">` with `pill`s on the right – they hide below 30rem window width) and, beside it, an optional `.row-actions` with a `control-button` dropdown (⋯: e.g. "Rolle löschen", red, opening a confirm dialog – or a dialog that explains why it can't be deleted). Below the head `<div class="accordion-body" hidden>`: the record's fields, each saving at once (`form[data-autosave]`, a name with the ✓ of the Feld mit Symbol, checkboxes in `fieldset`s, a foldable card `compact` for rarer parts, a Schalter), label/value lines as `ul.prop-list` > `li.prop-row` (`.prop-name`, `.prop-value`). A click on the head opens the body and closes the other open row of the list (`core/accordion.js`, delegated; event `accordion:toggle { row, open }`); the open row has the white marked look (`.is-open`). The server may draw a row open (`?open=<id>` after a create). A row that can't be opened (Admin, oneself) uses `<span class="accordion-static">` instead of the button. Adding is the Aufklapp-Plus als Zeile above the list. No save button, no stacked form. Reference: `roles/index.blade.php`, `members/index.blade.php`.
- **Initialen-Kreis (initials circle, point 218)** – `<span class="initials">MM</span>` in a person's row instead of a row symbol: the first letters of the first and last name in a 38 px circle, orange tint (`--primary` 16 %) with orange text; `initials is-paused` grey. CSS in `core/lists.css`.
- **Ajax into a card** – `<form data-ajax="#target">`: sends in the background, the controller answers JSON `{html}` with a partial, which replaces `#target`. Without JavaScript the form posts as usual.
- **Click-to-edit** – `<x-edit-in-place name="note" :action="route(…)" :value="…" multiline placeholder="…" label="…" />`: shows plain text with a quiet hover frame (grey placeholder when empty); click, or Tab + Enter, turns it into a field of the same size; blur or Ctrl+Enter saves (JSON POST, toast "Gespeichert."), Esc restores; the field while editing is `.edit-in-place-field`, `class="edit-in-place-small"` gives the small quiet variant (translation, recognised text). A refused value (422) shows the server's `message` in the error toast; after a save the framework emits `DocHamster.on('edit-in-place:saved', ({ box, json }) => …)`. Sticky notes are the model. **Feld mit ✓ (PO 2026-11-16):** only for a value that is already saved – at rest plain text (light grey on hover, no frame); a click turns it into a slim field with **✓ behind it** (`.edit-field`, `DocHamster.checkField(field, label, onApply)` in `core/edit-in-place.js`) – Enter, ✓ or leaving saves, Escape cancels, no X. The same look as the window title rename and the row rename. New input (sign-in, Eintragszeile, Neues Konto, search) keeps visible fields.
- **Feld mit Knopf (PO 2026-11-16)** – `<div class="field-row"><input …><button …>Weiter</button></div>`: a field and its button in one row – the same height (40 px), a fixed gap (.5rem), the field takes the room.
- **Field with symbol ("Feld mit Symbol", A195–A199)** – like input groups: a wrapper around one field with symbols inside it.
  ```html
  <div class="field">
      <label for="price">Betrag</label>
      <div class="field-addon">
          <span class="addon-lead" aria-hidden="true">€</span>            <!-- or an <x-icon> (@, search) -->
          <input id="price" inputmode="decimal">
          <span class="addon-end">
              <button type="button" class="control-button addon-button" data-field-save aria-label="Speichern" data-tooltip="Speichern" hidden><x-icon name="check" /></button>
          </span>
      </div>
  </div>
  ```
  `addon-lead`: a leading symbol or short text (muted, not clickable). `addon-end`: up to two parts on the right inside the field – a frameless button (`control-button addon-button`, always `aria-label` + tooltip): **✓ save** (`data-field-save`, see Properties module; a check, not a disk), **X clear** (`data-field-clear`, hides while empty), **eye** (`data-field-reveal` with both icons `eye` and `eye-off`, `aria-pressed`, `data-label-show`/`-hide`) – or a **state symbol** `<span class="addon-state" role="img" aria-label="…">`: `field-addon is-ok` = green ✓ and green frame, `is-error` = red ! (`alert-circle`) and red frame. The field keeps its look, focus ring and height (40 px); its padding makes room for each visible part. Our colours only.
- **Aufklapp-Plus (expanding plus, point 178, A354)** – adds a thing by name in one step, without a dialog (a notes column; later e.g. "+ Tag"):
  ```html
  <form class="expand-plus" data-expand-plus method="post" action="…">
      <button type="button" class="icon-button expand-plus-open" aria-label="Neue Spalte hinzufügen" data-tooltip="Neue Spalte hinzufügen"><x-icon name="plus" /></button>
      <div class="field-addon expand-plus-field">
          <input class="input-small" name="name" placeholder="Name der Spalte" aria-label="Name der Spalte">
          <span class="addon-end"><button type="submit" class="control-button addon-button" aria-label="Spalte anlegen" data-tooltip="Spalte anlegen"><x-icon name="check" /></button></span>
      </div>
  </form>
  ```
  At rest only the small plus. Hover, focus (Tab) or a tap open it into a small field with ✓ (a Feld mit Symbol); **Enter or ✓ sends** the form, **Escape** empties and folds it, an empty field folds when the pointer or focus leaves, a field with text or focus stays open; an empty field sends nothing. The module sets the open width with `--expand-plus-width` (default 16rem; notes: a column's 18rem). `core/expand-plus.js` (delegated, every window), CSS in `core/forms.css`. Without JavaScript hovering still opens it and the form posts.
  **Row variant (points 220, 221, A388) – the one way to add a record to a settings list:** no create form at the bottom of a page and no big orange button; the first line **above** the list is `<form class="expand-plus is-row" data-expand-plus method="post" action="…">` with `<button type="button" class="list-entry expand-plus-open"><x-icon name="plus" /><span>Eigenschaft hinzufügen</span></button>` and `<div class="pick-row is-static expand-plus-row">` holding the name field (`field-addon expand-plus-field`, its submit ✓ `hidden` – it shows only while a name is typed) and the record's other fields side by side, wrapping in a narrow window: an Umschalter als Auswahl (Art), a colour circle `input[type=color]`, a short `input-small` (a code), a `.field` taking a whole line. A click, tap or focus opens it (never hover); **Enter or ✓ creates**, **Escape** empties every field, puts the Umschalter back and folds it; a row with text stays open. `data-show-when="<field>:<value>"` shows a part only while that field has that value (Auswahlwerte only for Art = Auswahl); `data-expand-focus` on an earlier field takes the first focus. The page posts as usual; the controller puts the new record **first** in the list (the row closes on the reload). After a refused create the page renders it `is-open is-filled` with the old input. Users: Einstellungen → Eigenschaften, Farben, Bank → Kein Beleg nötig, Kartenkategorien.
- **Kopierfeld (copy field, points 152, A329)** – `<div class="field-addon is-copy"><input id="…" value="…" readonly><span class="addon-end"><button type="button" class="control-button addon-button" data-copy="<input id>" data-copied="Kopiert." aria-label="Kopieren" data-tooltip="Kopieren"><x-icon name="copy" /></button></span></div>` inside a `.field`: a fixed value to copy (a key, an address), monospace. The symbol copies the field's value and toasts `data-copied` (`core/dialogs.js`, delegated; a plain button with text instead swaps its text). **A key shown once** stands in a card with `.card-text` saying it is visible only now; afterwards a page shows only its first characters.
- **Colour picker** – `<x-color-picker name="color" :value="…" :allow-none :allow-own />` (renders `fieldset.tag-color-field` with `.tag-colors`, and `.tag-color-own` for the own colour): the organisation's palette (Einstellungen → Farben, 20 colours to start), optionally "keine Farbe" and an own colour. Notes, folders and tags all use it; an item keeps its hex when the palette changes. **PO 2026-11-16:** "Eigene Farbe" is one more circle at the end of the row (same size, a plus; filled with the colour once chosen) – a radio whose value is the colour, also sent as `<name>_hex`. A click opens our own dialog (`core/color-field.js`, `.color-dialog`): a colour area (`.color-sv`), a hue slider (`.color-hue`), then HEX, Rot, Grün, Blau one below the other (`.color-values`); "Übernehmen" sets it, "Abbrechen", X or Escape keep the old choice. Never the browser's colour picker, no #rrggbb field beside the circles.
- **Field filling a row** – `<form class="row-field">` inside a `pick-row` (rename in place with a `field-addon` ✓).
- **Upload by drop (point 85, A169–A175, point 181)** – no full-screen overlay any more. Files dragged over the page show a drop field "Loslassen: in den Posteingang" at the top of the main menu; over a folder row the row itself becomes the field "Loslassen zum Hochladen in „X“" (`data-upload-label`, class `is-upload-target`), over a folder window (inside a folder, not timeline/calendar) that window's body (→ its folder). **Anywhere else** – the drop field, the Posteingang, any other window's body ("… in „Posteingang“"), the free desk (the menu's drop field lights up) – the files go into the Posteingang **without a folder** (A358). The Papierkorb row is no target; a statement drop (Bank) and native file fields keep their own drop. Same orange as moving. Toast "3 Dokumente in „X“ hochgeladen".
